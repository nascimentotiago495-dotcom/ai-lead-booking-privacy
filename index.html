Privacy Policy

AI Lead Booking System

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Demonstration project: This application is a portfolio demonstration. Smile Dental is a fictional dental clinic. This system does not provide real dental appointments or medical services.

1. Purpose

This project demonstrates an AI-powered WhatsApp receptionist for responding to enquiries, qualifying leads and demonstrating appointment-booking workflows.

2. Information processed

During authorized testing, the system may process WhatsApp phone numbers, message content, conversation history and information voluntarily provided by testers.

Testers should not submit sensitive personal information, medical records or real patient information.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate and evaluate the demonstration chatbot, maintain conversation context, prevent duplicate processing and test integrations.

4. Third-party services

The demonstration uses Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, n8n and an AI service to process test conversations. Additional integrations, such as CRM and calendar services, may be introduced during development.

5. Data retention and deletion

Test information may be stored in workflow execution records and conversation tables during development. Testers may request deletion of their information using the contact details provided by the project owner.

6. Security

Access to the automation and connected services is restricted to authorized accounts. No system can guarantee absolute security.
